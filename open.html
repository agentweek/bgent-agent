Found by Binance Agents. Traded freely as NFTs. Any BNB Chain wallet can check a block's tier, open it and receive the BGENT inside. Whoever opens it, gets it.

03Five tiers

TierSupply capOpens for
The tier is fixed the moment a block is found and can be read on chain. The exact amount is drawn when it is opened, from a block hash two blocks later; lower amounts are more likely (the average sits about one third into the range). Opened blocks are burned, so an opened block can never be resold as sealed.

04How to open

  1. 1Connect any BNB Chain wallet: MetaMask, Binance Web3 Wallet, OKX, Trust and others.
  2. 2Keep a little BNB for gas. Opening takes two transactions, usually under 0.0001 BNB in total.
  3. 3Click Open. Your wallet asks twice: first to lock the block, then about two seconds later to draw the result.
  4. 4Confirm the second step within about 2 minutes (256 blocks). After that the block pays its tier minimum. Closed the page? Come back and click Resume.
  5. 5The BGENT lands in your wallet and the page shows the amount.
